# Changelog

## v1.2

- **Works on cards limited to 2048×2048 textures.** Chat/Items now turns on there with FFXI's high-resolution font setting on.
- **Commas look right.** Commas and semicolons in menus, names and nameplates keep their tail instead of a dark blob.
- **Damage numbers keep their dark edge.** The outline under round digits is no longer greyed out.
- **Periods stay visible with Shadow on.**

## v1.1

- XiView's high-resolution letter sheets (the game's layout at 2x or 4x) are supported (its 512 sheet confirmed in play); the panel says when a font mod's layout isn't recognised.

## v1.0

Initial release.
